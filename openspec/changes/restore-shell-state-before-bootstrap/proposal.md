## Why

WebChat must not make page rendering conditional on application readiness. Doing so removes the original `Header` / `Main` / `Footer` / conditional `Setup` / `Toaster` composition while initialization is pending and forces loading feedback into a separate surface outside the visual panel.

The Owner confirmed the final correction: freeze only the top-level component hierarchy and panel composition from release `v1.9.7`; preserve `StrictMode -> RemeshRoot -> RemeshScope -> App`, with the Scope's Domain list determined by the existing business needs rather than this change; keep `App` as the direct business owner without an `Application` middle layer or initialization props; and keep the original `AppMain` children and Toaster placement unchanged. Historical whole-tree rendering gates are not frozen: neither initialization `ready` nor `appStatusLoadIsFinished` may gate this composition.

## What Changes

- Freeze the `v1.9.7` content-root component shape `StrictMode -> RemeshRoot(store) -> RemeshScope(existing required Domains) -> App`, with `<App />` receiving no initialization dependencies and no initialization wrapper, `Application` middle layer, alternate root, extra root-level Scope, fallback tree, or UI phase. This change SHALL NOT prescribe a new Domain list or freeze historical whole-tree rendering gates.
- Preserve the `v1.9.7` `App` component order inside themed `#app`: `AppMain` then `AppButton`, with `Header`, `Main`, `Footer`, conditional `Setup`, then `Toaster` as `AppMain` children, followed by `DanmakuContainer` at the app level. Remove `appStatusLoadIsFinished` and every initialization `ready` condition around this component tree. Readiness MAY gate only a specific Runtime-dependent business operation at its use site.
- Let business components such as `App`, `AppMain`, and `AppButton` consume their own hooks, stores, Domains, and services at the use site. Do not pass initialization functions, activation callbacks, business-state bundles, or test-only timing controls through them, and do not replace prop drilling with a Provider, context, controller, or injection wrapper. Among WebChat-owned components, only pure presentation components under `components` receive the minimal display props they need.
- Mount the normal `AppMain` plus `AppButton` tree before the existing `AppStatus` read or any application initialization dependency settles. Hydration/persistence SHALL restore the saved expanded or collapsed value independently; a user choice accepted while the read is pending SHALL win over the older stored snapshot.
- Restore exactly one generic Toaster to the original application fragment: it is the direct React sibling of `Header`, `Main`, `Footer`, and conditional `Setup`, and therefore a DOM descendant of the positioned `AppMain` visual panel. It SHALL NOT be passed to `AppMain`, hoisted beside that panel, mounted in another root/portal, or externalized to stay visible while the panel is collapsed.
- Represent active initialization and its current terminal failure only through the existing `Toast.ts` loading/error commands, retaining `Preparing WebChat` for loading and `WebChat unavailable` for the normalized error. Remove every independent loading, unavailable, error, result, or Retry status component from the panel.
- Keep the required initialization sequence, deadlines, cancellation, single-flight attempt identity, stale-result fencing, and Runtime detach behavior as non-presentational lifecycle logic. Initialization MAY gate only capabilities that depend on it and SHALL never gate or replace the shell.
- Keep the existing AppButton actions menu reachable before readiness. Its existing Refresh slot SHALL retry the whole initialization while dependent application capabilities are not ready; after readiness, the same slot SHALL retain its existing current-site ChatRoom retry/reconnect behavior.
- Project the current accepted operation through the same Refresh control: disabled and rotating while its initialization or ready-application operation is in flight, duplicate activations rejected, stale generations fenced, and ordinary eligibility restored at the matching terminal. Success SHALL publish no success Toast.
- Preserve one `AppStatus` owner and the one existing `Toast.ts` capability across failure, Retry, and readiness. Delete `ToastPresentation.ts` and `toast-presentation.tsx`; if stable IDs or presentation options are required, extend `Toast.ts` minimally instead of adding another Domain/adapter. A pre-hydration user expand/collapse SHALL win over an older stored snapshot, and old document or initialization work SHALL not overwrite the current shell state.
- Add deterministic controls proving the frozen `v1.9.7` root/App/AppMain hierarchy, prop-free `App`, absence of an `Application` middle layer or initialization-ready composition branch, Toaster ancestry inside the positioned panel, use-site business dependencies, absence of replacement injection, context-sensitive Refresh, dependency gating, single-flight operation, and stale-result protection. Tests SHALL use the fixed stack: `vitest`, `happy-dom`, the complete `@testing-library/*` and `@vitest/*` families as needed, and `vitest-browser-react`; `linkedom` is not part of that stack. Stable `data-testid` selectors MAY be written directly on existing production JSX; dynamic injection, test-only props/wrappers, and runtime DOM rewriting are forbidden.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `webrtc-runtime`: Freeze the `v1.9.7` component composition across initialization, remove whole-tree state gates, carry status through its panel-owned generic Toaster, consume business dependencies at their use sites, and restore persisted shell state independently of initialization.

## Impact

- Affected implementation after authority approval: restoration of the `v1.9.7` content/App/AppMain component composition, business dependency ownership, removal of whole-page `appStatusLoadIsFinished` and initialization-ready rendering gates, initialization orchestration, shell-lifetime `AppStatus`, original panel-owned generic Toast placement, AppButton actions-menu availability, context-sensitive Refresh dispatch, and focused regressions.
- Affected behavior: refresh preserves the frozen normal page composition, restores the saved expanded/collapsed choice independently of every initialization outcome, displays initialization loading/failure only through the original panel Toaster, and retries initialization from AppButton Refresh without any alternate status view.
- Superseded behavior: any clause that preserves an initialization-specific loading shell, an `Application` middle layer, a wrapper or `ready` branch that creates page content only after initialization succeeds, a Toaster beside/outside the visual panel, or dependency/test-injection props on business components.
- Unchanged: initialization dependency order and deadlines; dependency-based capability gating; the `AppStatus` storage key/record/version and no-record default; unread/position semantics; ready-state ChatRoom Refresh scope; Runtime/protocol/public APIs; generic Toast accessibility, default lifetime, and no-success-Toast policies; visual theme/configuration; browser permissions and production dependencies. Test-only development dependencies MAY change only to align with the fixed test stack.
- The Chromium cross-world preload warning, mandatory initialization-stage logging, timeout redesign, raw diagnostic copy, new error page, automatic opening, and visual redesign are outside this change.
- QA, QC, and UX are not part of this task unless the Owner later requests them explicitly. Merge follows the established acceptance and authorization flow.
